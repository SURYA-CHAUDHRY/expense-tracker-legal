Delete your account and data

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Privacy Policy Home

You can permanently delete your Expense Tracker account and the cloud backup stored for it from inside the app. If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, you can ask us to delete the data by email.

Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Expense Tracker and make sure you are signed in with the Google account you want to delete.
  2. On the Home screen, tap the Settings icon (top right).
  3. Tap Backup & sign-in.
  4. Tap Delete account and all data, then Delete everything.
  5. Confirm with the same Google account when the Google account chooser appears.

The app asks Google to confirm it is really you before deleting anything. If Google refuses or a different account is chosen, nothing is deleted and you can try again.

This cannot be undone. The in-app option permanently deletes:
  • your Google sign-in for the app (the Firebase account)
  • your entire cloud backup: transactions, budgets, categories, accounts and recurring bills
  • the same data on the device where you complete the deletion
No copy is kept by the app.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the in-app option, email heyitssurya.dev@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Expense Tracker data and send it from the Google account you used in the app. This helps us find the right backup. We may ask you to verify that you control the account.

We will complete verified requests within 30 days and reply when the data has been deleted.

If you used the app as a guest

Guest use is entirely local, so there is no account or cloud backup to delete. To remove the data from your phone, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Other devices

Deleting your account removes the cloud backup and the sign-in itself. Local data already copied to another device is not erased remotely; sign out and clear the app's storage on that device if you no longer want it there.

What is kept

The app keeps nothing after deletion. Google Firebase and Google Cloud may retain limited operational logs or residual copies for a short period under Google's own terms and legal obligations. We do not keep a separate copy of your backup.
