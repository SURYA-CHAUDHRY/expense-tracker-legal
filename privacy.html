This policy explains what the Expense Tracker Android app ("the app", "we", "us") does with your information. It applies to the app published on Google Play. The short version: the app works without an account and keeps your data on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy is backed up to Google Firebase so you can restore it later.

In one sentence: no ads, no analytics, no selling of data. Guest use is entirely local; signing in adds a private cloud backup.

1. Information the app collects

If you use the app without signing in, the app does not send your expense data anywhere. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Transactions (amounts, dates, notes, merchant names, categories, payment method)
  • Budgets, recurring bills, custom categories and accounts you create
  • App settings such as the month start day and notification preferences
  • Your app-lock PIN, stored only as a salted hash on the device

Notifications are scheduled locally by the Android system. Biometric unlock, if enabled, is handled entirely by your device; the app never receives your fingerprint or face data.

If you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your name, email address, profile picture URL and Google/Firebase user ID
  • A backup of the data listed above: transactions, budgets, recurring bills, categories and accounts

Signing in is optional and is used only for cloud backup, restore and account management. Settings such as your app-lock PIN and merchant-matching rules are not uploaded.

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) may process technical information such as your IP address, app instance identifiers and diagnostic information as part of providing those services.

2. Information the app does not collect

  • No advertising, ad identifiers or advertising partners
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or SMS access
  • No selling or renting of personal information to anyone

3. How the information is used

  • To store and display your expenses, budgets and bills on your device
  • To back up and restore your data when you sign in
  • To authenticate you and to let you delete your account and its backup
  • To send local notifications you have enabled (for example, bill reminders)
  • To respond to support or deletion requests you send us

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing is based on performance of the service you requested (backup and restore) and, for optional features, your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by signing out, deleting your account or uninstalling the app.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your information. Cloud features rely on Google Firebase (Google LLC), which acts as a service provider and processes data on Google's infrastructure. Google Sign-In is used to authenticate you. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Storage, security and international transfers

Local data is stored in the app's private database on your device. The database is not encrypted at rest: the app lock is a privacy screen, not encryption, and someone with access to your device's app storage could read it. Cloud data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored in Google Cloud Firestore, tied to your signed-in account.

Google may process data in countries other than yours, using the safeguards described in Google's privacy terms.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Cloud data remains until you delete your account in the app or ask us to delete it. Deleting your account removes the cloud backup and the sign-in account. See Delete your account and data for the exact steps.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, or to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. You can exercise the deletion and access options in the app, or contact us at the address below. We aim to respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
